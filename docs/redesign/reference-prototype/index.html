dragon’s favorite lollipop

Melt 1 cup sugar with 1/3 cup corn syrup and 2 tbsp water over medium heat. Do not stir. At 300°F add a drop of teal food coloring and a pinch of chili flakes (dragons like it spicy). Pour into molds, insert sticks, and let cool. Yields 12 lollipops. Hide at least 3 from the dragon.
